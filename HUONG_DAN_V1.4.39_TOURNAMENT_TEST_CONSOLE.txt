PES ARENA V1.4.39 - TOURNAMENT TEST CONSOLE
============================================

Muc dich
- Kiem tra toan bo giai dau duoi goc nhin HLV ma khong can HLV/tai khoan/tran dau that.
- Sandbox Test Mode tach biet, khong ghi Rank/RP/Zcoin va khong sua giai that.

Cach dung nhanh
1. Vao Admin -> Giai dau -> Tournament Test Mode.
2. Khuyen nghi chon 36 HLV, GĐ1 5/6 tran, 3/4 Pot.
3. Tai muc "Chon HLV + nhay giai doan", bam truc tiep:
   - Dang ky
   - GĐ1
   - GĐ2 League
   - Play-off
   - Vong 1/8
   - Tu ket
   - Ban ket
   - Chung ket
4. Neu khong chon HLV, he thong tu chon mot HLV phu hop voi vong do.
5. Trong man hinh HLV Test, co the doi HLV hoac doi giai doan ngay tren thanh TEST MODE.

Cac noi dung co the kiem tra
- Dang ky: Host, Bac/Trung/Nam, Zalo, QR, noi dung CK "Ten HLV C1 Arena", cho Admin duyet.
- GĐ1: doi thu, tien do, ket qua, BXH.
- GĐ2: Pot, doi thu League, BXH va trang thai Top 8 / 9-24 / bi loai.
- Play-off: cap dau hai luot cua hang 9-24.
- 1/8 -> Ban ket: chi HLV con trong giai moi co cap dau ca nhan.
- Chung ket: Bo3; tran 1/2 90 phut, tran 3 co ET/PEN.

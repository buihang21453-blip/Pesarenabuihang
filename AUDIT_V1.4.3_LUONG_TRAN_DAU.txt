Ngay: 2026-08-09

STATE MACHINE CHUAN
1. Moi dau
   match_invites.status = pending
   match_rooms.status = waiting_ready
   host trong phong, guest chua vao

2. Doi thu chap nhan
   match_invites.status = accepted
   match_rooms.guest_user_id = nguoi nhan
   match_rooms.guest_ready = false

3. San sang
   match_rooms.status = waiting_ready
   match_rooms.guest_ready = true
   Backend phai xac nhan DB write thanh cong moi bao thanh cong tren UI.

4. Quay quan
   Tao matches.status = playing
   Sau do noi match_rooms.match_id -> matches.id va room.status = playing
   Neu cap nhat room that bai thi xoa match vua tao de khong tao du lieu mo coi.

5. Gui ket qua
   matches.status: playing -> waiting_confirm
   match_rooms.status: playing -> waiting_result_confirm
   Neu room write that bai thi rollback matches ve playing.

6. Xac nhan
   matches.status -> confirmed + RP
   match_rooms.status -> confirmed
   KHONG nhay thang ve waiting_ready.
   Giu doi, ty so, match_id de UI hien thi ket qua va mo nut Da tiep / Roi phong.

7. Da tiep
   Nguoi bam dau: room.status van confirmed, note ghi nguoi da dong y.
   Nguoi bam thu hai: room.status -> waiting_ready, guest_ready = true,
   xoa doi/ty so/match_id cu va giu nguyen che do.
   Host va Guest dung cung mot logic, khong con truong hop Guest bam truoc tu mo tran moi.

8. Thoat phong
   Truoc khi san sang: room_leave an toan.
   Sau khi da san sang / dang choi: route forfeit, ap dung penalty theo quy tac hien tai.
   Sau confirmed: room_rematch_decline, phong khong con khoa nguoi choi va doi thu duoc thong bao.

9. Auto-confirm 60 giay
   Cung state machine voi xac nhan thu cong:
   waiting_result_confirm -> confirmed -> Da tiep / Roi phong.
   Khong con tu dong bo qua confirmed.

10. Series
   Tran con chua ket thuc Series: waiting_result_confirm -> waiting_ready de vao tran con tiep.
   Series hoan tat: waiting_result_confirm -> confirmed -> Da tiep / Roi phong.

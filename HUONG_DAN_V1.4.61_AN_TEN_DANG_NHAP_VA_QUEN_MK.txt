PES Arena V1.4.61

1. Bao mat ten dang nhap
- User thuong chi thay Ten hien thi cua tai khoan khac.
- Trang Players khong hien username va khong tim theo username doi voi user thuong.
- Ho so nguoi khac khong hien username.
- Chinh chu van thay username cua minh trong Quan ly ho so.
- Admin van thay username day du trong khu vuc Admin va khi can quan tri.

2. Quen mat khau
- Nhap Ten tai khoan + SDT Zalo dang dung.
- SDT Zalo KHONG doi chieu voi tai khoan; chi luu vao lich su yeu cau de Admin biet thong tin lien he.
- Neu Ten tai khoan ton tai, he thong tu cap mat khau tam 6 ky tu ngau nhien.
- Mat khau tam bat buoc doi sau khi dang nhap.
- Gioi han yeu cau lien tuc van duoc giu.

3. Dang ky tai khoan
- Khong bat buoc nhap SDT Zalo khi tao tai khoan.
- SDT Zalo khong con la thuoc tinh ho so tai khoan.
